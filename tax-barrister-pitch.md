# Pitch to Tax Barrister — AI Automation for Law Firm

## The Problem (Frame it in his language)

"Sir, I've noticed most top tax law firms have two problems that cost them millions:

1. **Senior barristers** earning ₦500k-₦2M/hour are reviewing documents junior staff should handle with AI — this burns billable hours
2. **Clients** call repeatedly asking 'what's the update?' because there's no automated follow-up — this costs referrals

Most firms solve this by hiring more junior staff. But that adds payroll, training, and management overhead.

I build AI automation systems that do the junior work in seconds. Your senior people focus on high-value legal strategy. Your clients get answered without lifting a finger."

---

## The Simple Demo (What you show him TODAY)

Build this in n8n in 2 hours and demo on your laptop:

**Workflow 1 — Client Document Intake & Confirmation**
- He gives you a sample client document
- You upload it → AI reads it → extracts client name, tax year, document type → sends auto-SMS to client: "We've received your 2024 tax documents. A barrister will review within 48 hours."
- Looks impressive, takes 2 hours to build

**Workflow 2 — Tax Law Change Monitor**
- Scrapes FIRS website or any tax news source daily
- AI summarizes: "What changed, who it affects, action needed"
- Emails him a 3-bullet summary every morning

---

## The Full System (What you propose)

### Phase 1 — Quick Win (Week 1-2) — ₦500k
- Client intake automation (documents → AI extracts → auto-files → SMS client)
- Missed call SMS for the firm's office line
- Daily tax news briefing email

### Phase 2 — Intelligence (Week 3-6) — ₦1.5M
- AI document analyzer: upload any financial doc → structured summary + flag anomalies
- Client status portal: every client gets a link they can check anytime ("where is my case?")
- Auto-reminders for tax filing deadlines for each client

### Phase 3 — Scale (Week 7-12) — ₦3M+
- AI that drafts initial responses to tax queries
- Integration with his existing case management software
- Dashboard showing all client activity, bottlenecks, revenue pipeline

## Pricing Strategy

- **Phase 1:** ₦500,000 (≈ $300) — cheap enough to say yes on the spot
- **Phase 2:** ₦1,500,000 (≈ $900) — after he's seen Phase 1 work
- **Monthly maintenance:** ₦200,000 (≈ $120/mo) — hosting, updates, support

Or in naira terms a billionaire can relate to:
- "Phase 1 costs less than one week of a junior associate's salary"
- "Phase 2 costs less than one client complaint that leads to a bad review"

---

## Key Principles

1. **Never mention the tech** — he doesn't care about n8n, webhooks, or GPT
2. **Speak in law firm terms** — billable hours, client retention, document review, deadlines
3. **Show, don't tell** — bring a working demo, not slides
4. **Price in value, not cost** — saving 10 hours/week of a senior barrister at ₦500k/hr = ₦5M/week saved. Your ₦500k fee is nothing compared to that
5. **Start small** — get a yes on Phase 1, deliver perfectly, THEN propose Phase 2
