SECTION 03
Step 01
Define the Subject
Who or what is in the frame. Be specific about age, appearance, clothing, expression.
"Weathered male explorer, 40s, dirt-streaked face"
Step 02
Set the Action
What is the subject doing? Use verbs that imply motion the AI can animate.
"slowly raising a brass lantern, scanning the darkness"
Step 03
Add Environment + Light
Where are they? What's the lighting? This grounds the AI in physical space.
"ancient stone cave, chiaroscuro, lantern as only light source"
