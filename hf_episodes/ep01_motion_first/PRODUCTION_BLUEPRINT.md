## GLOBAL DESIGN LANGUAGE

**Color palette:**
- Default background: `#080808`
- Problem / danger: `#c0392b`
- Solution / clarity: `#2980e8`
- Caution / warning: `#e8a020`
- Verdict screen: white `#ffffff`, text `#080808`
- Accent glow: `#00d4ff`
- Motion-first confirm: `#00e5a0`

**Typography:**
- Primary: Inter (800 for titles, 400-500 for body)
- Terminal/prompt: JetBrains Mono
- Small editorial: small-caps, wide tracking

**Animation rules:**
- Springs everywhere. Default: damping 18, stiffness 120
- Mechanical snap: damping 200
- Chaos animated chaotically. Precision animated precisely.
- Multi-element reveals stagger 4-6 frames minimum
- After major reveal: hold 1.5s minimum
- Palette shifts: hard cuts, not crossfades
- Text never just fades in — types on, scales from zero, slides, or assembles
