# Episode 1 — Creative Direction (Narrative-Driven)
# Every frame ILLUSTRATES what the narrator is saying.
# Flat design. Clean geometry. Bold typography. Visual metaphors.

## Visual Style
- Background: #F5F0EB (warm off-white, NOT black)
- Primary text: #1A1A1A (near-black)
- Accent 1: #2563EB (blue — correct/good)
- Accent 2: #DC2626 (red — wrong/bad)
- Accent 3: #7C3AED (purple — neutral/info)
- Style: Flat design, clean shapes, bold sans-serif typography
- NO neon, NO dark backgrounds, NO abstract particle art

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (6s)
Script: "If you are copying and pasting your image prompts into an AI video generator, you are ruining your video"
Visual: Split screen. Left side shows a cursor selecting text from a prompt box labeled "Midjourney Prompt". Right side shows a video player with a glitching, morphing mess. A big red "X" stamps over the video.
Mood: Problem identification. Something is wrong.

## Scene 2 — The Double-Prompting Trap (7s)
Script: "Most people jump into Midjourney... spend an hour crafting a hyper-detailed prompt... copy that exact same wall of text into Runway..."
Visual: LEFT: A detailed prompt box with 200+ words of dense text (leather jacket, volumetric lighting, rain-slicked streets...). An arrow points RIGHT to a video player showing a face morphing/distorting. The text wall is too long — it overflows the box.
Mood: Overwhelm. Too much text = bad results.

## Scene 3 — The Paradigm Shift (6s)
Script: "We have to shift to a motion-first mindset. That image already carries 100% of the composition..."
Visual: A large toggle switch in center. Left side says "DESCRIBE EVERYTHING" (red). Right side says "MOTION ONLY" (green). The toggle flips from left to right. Two clean icons appear: a camera icon and a clock icon.
Mood: Clarity. The switch moment.

## Scene 4 — Camera Movement (5s)
Script: "Step one is Camera Movement. Use clear terms like 'slow pan right,' 'cinematic zoom in,' or 'low-angle crane shot.'"
Visual: A simple camera icon in the center. Three labeled arrows point outward: "SLOW PAN →", "ZOOM IN ⟐", "CRANE SHOT ↑". Clean, diagram-style.
Mood: Educational. Clear instruction.

## Scene 5 — Subject Action (5s)
Script: "Step two is Subject Action. Keep this minimal. Instead of 'man runs furiously,' try 'the man slowly turns his head.'"
Visual: Side-by-side. LEFT (red border): "man runs furiously" with a chaotic scribble figure. RIGHT (green border): "man slowly turns head" with a calm, simple figure turning. Clean comparison.
Mood: Before/after. Bad vs good.

## Scene 6 — Environmental Physics (5s)
Script: "Step three is Environmental Physics. This describes subtle, secondary motion like 'leaves rustling' or 'smoke drifting.'"
Visual: A simple landscape silhouette (mountains, tree). Small elements float: leaves drifting, smoke wisps, dust particles. All subtle, gentle motion. Clean, minimal.
Mood: Subtle. Atmosphere.

## Scene 7 — The Formula (8s)
Script: "Our formula skips the description and focuses only on the movement: 'Slow push-in. Explorer raises lantern. Dust motes float.'"
Visual: Split screen. LEFT (red, cluttered): "A rugged explorer with a dirty face holds a glowing lantern inside a dark, wet stone cave, cinematic lighting" — wall of text. RIGHT (green, clean): Three clean lines: "1. Slow push-in" "2. Explorer raises lantern" "3. Dust motes float". The right side GLOWS.
Mood: The reveal. The solution.

## Scene 8 — Limitations (6s)
Script: "AI video still struggles with complex, high-velocity physical interactions like tying shoelaces."
Visual: A simple hand icon trying to tie shoelaces. The fingers distort and morph into a tangled mess. A yellow warning triangle overlays. Clean, minimal.
Mood: Honest. Limitations.

## Scene 9 — Verdict (6s)
Script: "I highly recommend adopting the motion-first methodology immediately"
Visual: A large, clean circular seal stamp. Text: "MOTION-FIRST: APPROVED" in bold. Simple laurel wreath around it. Stamps down with impact. Clean white/cream background.
Mood: Authoritative. Final.

## Scene 10 — CTA (6s)
Script: "Subscribe to Sylvester's AI Lab for straightforward, hype-free AI tutorials"
Visual: Clean centered text "SYLVESTER'S AI LAB" with a simple beaker icon. Below: a subscribe button that pulses. Minimal.
Mood: Clean. Brand.
