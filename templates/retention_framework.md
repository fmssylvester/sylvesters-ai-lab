# YouTube High-Retention Script Framework

## 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)
- Rule: Direct payoff promise within the first 5 seconds.
- Structure: 
  1. Visual & Verbal Stinger (What we are building/solving).
  2. The Stakes (Why existing methods fail or take too long).
  3. The Proof (Brief glimpse of the working n8n workflow or end result).

## 2. Avatar Pattern Interrupt Breaks
- Rule: Insert an avatar talking-head segment every 60-90 seconds or during major technical transitions.
- Purpose: Re-engage audience attention, deliver high-energy conceptual explanations, and transition into the next practical block.

## 3. Channel Outro & Call to Action
- Rule: No sudden drop-offs. Seamlessly bridge the end of the tutorial into the channel asset loop.
- Structure:
  1. Direct value recap (1 sentence).
  2. CTA for free template download/Gumroad link.
  3. Call-to-action driving viewers to the next related video on the channel.
