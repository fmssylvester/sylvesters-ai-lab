# B-Roll Prompts for Sylvester's AI Lab

All prompts are written for AI video generators (Seedance 2.0, Runway, Kling, etc.).
Each prompt targets 5-10 seconds of footage at 16:9, 24fps minimum.

---

## SCENE: THE COLLECTOR

**Story**: Someone obsessively bookmarking AI tools they never actually use.

### Clip 1: "The Bookmark Session" (opening shot)
```
Cinematic close-up of a person's hands on a laptop keyboard in a dimly lit room.
The screen reflects a warm cyan glow onto their face. They are rapidly clicking
and scrolling. Camera slowly pushes in from medium to close-up. Shallow depth
of field, the background is soft bokeh of desk items. Moody, dark atmosphere
with a single desk lamp providing warm side light. 8 seconds.
```

### Clip 2: "The Accumulation" (montage feel)
```
Top-down view of a laptop screen showing a browser with 50+ open tabs. The
tabs are so small they're illegible. The person's hand enters frame to open
yet another tab. Camera slowly zooms into the tab bar. The screen light casts
a cold blue glow. Minimal, clean desk surface visible around the laptop.
Dark moody lighting. 6 seconds.
```

### Clip 3: "The Overwhelm" (emotional beat)
```
Medium shot of a young man sitting at a desk, leaning back in his chair,
rubbing his eyes with both hands. Multiple monitor setup visible, screens
showing various AI tool interfaces. He looks exhausted and overwhelmed.
Dark room, screen glow as primary light source. Cinematic color grading
with teal shadows and warm highlights. 5 seconds.
```

### Clip 4: "The Abandonment" (closing shot)
```
Slow cinematic push-in on a laptop screen showing a bookmarks folder
labeled "AI Tools" with 40+ saved items. The cursor hovers over the folder
but does not click. The screen reflects in the person's still face. Shallow
depth of field. The room is dark except for the screen glow. Melancholic,
contemplative mood. 7 seconds.
```

## GENERAL B-ROLL LIBRARY (reusable across all scenes)

### Category A: Person at Computer (various angles)

#### A1: Wide establishing shot
```
Wide cinematic shot of a person sitting at a modern desk setup in a dark room.
Three monitors glowing with different content. The person is focused, typing.
Backlit silhouette with rim light. Plants and shelves visible in soft focus
background. Moody, premium atmosphere. Camera slowly dollies right. 8 seconds.
```

#### A2: Over-the-shoulder
```
Over-the-shoulder shot of a person working on a computer. The screen shows
a clean interface with cards and text. The person's hand moves the mouse.
Shallow depth of field, the person's shoulder and head are soft in foreground.
Screen is sharp and readable. Dark room, screen provides the light. 6 seconds.
```

#### A3: Profile shot
```
Cinematic profile shot of a young man wearing glasses, illuminated by the
blue glow of a monitor. He is focused, occasionally typing. Dark background
with subtle bokeh of desk items. Camera is static, shallow depth of field.
Tech-forward, contemplative mood. 7 seconds.
```

#### A4: Hands close-up
```
Extreme close-up of hands typing on a mechanical keyboard. The keys have
subtle RGB backlighting in cyan. Fingers move deliberately, not rushed.
Shallow depth of field, only the current keys being pressed are sharp.
Dark environment. 5 seconds.
```

### Category B: Screen Interactions

#### B1: Scrolling through content
```
Screen recording style shot of a browser scrolling through a grid of AI
tool cards. Each card has an icon and short description. The scroll is
smooth and continuous, showing dozens of tools passing by. Clean dark
UI. Camera slightly angled, not perfectly flat. 6 seconds.
```

#### B2: Clicking and exploring
```
Close-up of a cursor clicking on a card in a dark-themed web interface.
The card expands with a smooth animation. The person scrolls down to
read more. Clean, minimal UI with cyan accent colors. Screen capture
aesthetic but filmed at a slight angle with shallow DOF. 5 seconds.
```

#### B3: Multiple windows
```
Shot of a monitor showing 4 browser windows tiled in a grid. Each window
shows a different AI tool interface. The person alt-tabs between them
rapidly. The screen content is slightly blurred due to the speed of
switching. Dark room, monitor glow. 5 seconds.
```

### Category C: Emotional Beats

#### C1: Frustration
```
Medium close-up of a person pinching the bridge of their nose, eyes closed.
They are sitting at a desk with monitors behind them. The screen light casts
shadows across their face. A sigh of frustration. Dark, moody lighting.
Cinematic. 4 seconds.
```

#### C2: Discovery moment
```
Close-up of a person's face lighting up with a slight smile as they look
at the screen. The monitor reflects in their glasses. The light on their
face shifts from cool blue to warm as if something exciting appeared on
screen. Subtle, natural reaction. 4 seconds.
```

#### C3: Decision paralysis
```
Shot from behind a person sitting between two monitors. Both screens show
different AI tools. The person's head turns slightly left, then right,
then back. They don't click anything. The cursor hovers motionless.
Tension in stillness. Dark room. 5 seconds.
```

### Category D: Abstract/Conceptual

#### D1: Data flow
```
Abstract cinematic shot of luminous cyan data streams flowing through a
dark void. The streams branch and merge, forming temporary connections
before dispersing. Like neurons firing or data packets routing. Smooth,
hypnotic motion. Dark background. 8 seconds.
```

#### D2: Network forming
```
Abstract shot of glowing nodes appearing one by one in a dark space.
Lines connect them as they appear, forming a growing network. The nodes
pulse with soft light. Cyan and gold accents. The network grows from
sparse to dense. Clean, minimal, premium feel. 8 seconds.
```

#### D3: Collapse
```
Abstract shot of a dense network of glowing nodes and connections slowly
dissolving. Nodes dim and fade, connections break and drift apart. The
network shrinks from complex to empty. Melancholic, clean. Dark void
background. 7 seconds.
```

### Category E: Environment/Establishing

#### E1: Desk setup beauty shot
```
Slow cinematic pan across a premium desk setup. Mechanical keyboard with
subtle lighting, ultrawide monitor, small plant, coffee mug, minimal
decor. The monitor shows a dark-themed interface. Warm desk lamp in
background. Shallow depth of field. Premium, aspirational tech aesthetic.
8 seconds.
```

#### E2: Room establishing
```
Wide shot of a modern home office at night. Desk with dual monitors,
chair pushed back slightly. The room is lit only by screen glow and a
small ambient light. Plants in corners. Clean, organized space. The
camera slowly pushes in toward the desk. Atmospheric, cinematic. 7 seconds.
```

## PROMPT TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

1. **Always specify**: "cinematic", "dark room", "screen glow", "shallow depth of field"
2. **For consistency**: Keep the same person/appearance across clips in a scene
3. **For our color palette**: Add "teal and cyan glow" or "warm amber accents" to match our tokens
4. **Camera movement**: Always specify — "static", "slow push-in", "dolly right", etc.
5. **Duration**: 5-8 seconds per clip is ideal for AI generators
6. **Resolution**: Request 1920x1080 or 1280x720 minimum
7. **Negative prompts** (if supported): "text overlay, watermarks, logos, faces distorted"
